Skyvant Eagle

Skyvant Eagle is a high-speed surveillance drone engineered for long-endurance operations and real-time situational awareness.

Designed for defense and strategic monitoring applications, Eagle delivers reliable performance across diverse terrains and operational conditions.

Key Specifications

01

Endurance

Capable of sustained flight for up to 50 minutes, ensuring extended surveillance and operational coverage.

02

Operational Range

Long-range mission capability with an operational range of up to 100 km.

03

RTK Navigation Support

RTK (Real-Time Kinematic) supported for positioning centimeter-level accuracy and precise mission execution.

04

Camera Payload Support

Compatible with camera payloads up to 400 g, supporting high-resolution imaging and surveillance systems.

05

Triple Redundancy System

Triple-layer redundancy across critical subsystems to ensure fail-operational performance in high-risk environments.

06

Fail-Safe Enabled

Built-in fail-safe mechanisms for automatic recovery, return, or safe landing during system anomalies.

07

In House Flight Controller

Equipped with a fully indigenous flight controller ensuring operational sovereignty and security.

08

Own Ground Control Station

Integrated with a proprietary ground control station for enhanced mission planning and real-time monitoring.

09

Customizable

The modular design and technology can be customized to meet specific mission requirements and payload integrations.
